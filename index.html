Standard Onboarding Steps

Follow these standard procedures for every new client registered:

  1. Verify contact details and create the client folder.
  2. Schedule and record the Kickoff Video Call.
  3. Issue system credentials and mark status as Access Granted.
  4. Confirm client setup sign-off and update stage to Completed.
