About TrackPilot

TrackPilot is a dashboard for tracking a client's progress through onboarding, from a signed contract to a fully completed setup.

The onboarding workflow

Every client moves through the same four stages. The dashboard groups clients by their current stage so it's easy to see who needs attention next.

  1. Contract Signed — the client has agreed to work with us and signed off.
  2. Kickoff Call — an initial call to align on scope, timeline, and expectations.
  3. Access Granted — the client has shared the accounts or access needed to start work.
  4. Completed — onboarding is finished and the client is fully set up.
