System Overview & Standard Operating Procedures

System Overview

TrackPilot is a web-based client management utility designed to reduce onboarding friction and maintain accountability across technical ops teams.

Standard Operating Procedures

Follow this sequential process for every new client onboarded:

  1. Verify contact details and register the client via the Dashboard Form.
  2. Schedule and conduct the preliminary Kickoff Video Call.
  3. Issue system access credentials and set status to Access Granted.
  4. Obtain final sign-off and mark the record as Completed.
